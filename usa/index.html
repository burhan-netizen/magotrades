People find you on Google, see your actual work and call you directly, with no one else bidding against you. And it keeps working without a monthly bill.

Questions from the USA

Straight answers.

How much does a contractor website cost?

From $799, paid once. That includes six pages, custom design, all the copy, on-page SEO, Google Business Profile setup, a quote form, tap-to-call, your Instagram feed and a full year of edits. Extra pages, more service areas or online booking get quoted before you commit. There's no monthly fee.

You're in India. Is that a problem for a US contractor?

Most clients find it's a perk. We work over WhatsApp, so you can send photos and voice notes straight from the job site. I reply quickly during your business hours, and because of the time difference, a lot of the work gets done overnight. You often wake up to a new preview waiting.

Will the wording sound American?

Yes. I write in American English: color, estimate, ZIP code, licensed and insured. I use the phrases homeowners type into Google, like "deck builder near me" or "foundation repair Plano". If a line ever sounds off to you, tell me and I'll rewrite it. It should sound like your business, because it is.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain is in your name and the code belongs to you. No lock-in contract, no monthly rent. If you ever want to move it, you can take it with you. After year one, you can leave it alone and pay nothing, or hire me for changes at $25 an hour.

How quickly can my site go live?

Send your Instagram or current website on WhatsApp and you'll get a free working preview within 48 hours, no strings attached. If you like it, we spend the next ten days or so on your photos, services and service areas while I write the copy. Most sites launch on your domain around day 14.
