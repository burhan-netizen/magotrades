The problem

Why glaziers lose jobs online.

01

Emergencies can't reach you

A broken window after a break-in is stressful. If your site doesn't put your after-hours number right up top, people call whichever glazier answers first, and that's rarely the one buried in a menu.

02

Glass is hard to photograph

Reflections and glare make glass work look flat or invisible in photos. Without a gallery that shows the finished product properly, customers can't appreciate the quality of a frameless install.

03

Commercial clients look elsewhere

Builders, shop owners and strata or property managers want a glazier who looks capable of bigger jobs. A small residential-only site tells them you're not the right fit, even when you are.

Questions glaziers ask

Straight answers.

How does SEO work for glaziers?

Glass searches are very product specific, so I build a page for each one: shower screens, pool fencing, splashbacks, glass replacement and so on, all tied to your service areas. The site is fast, the schema marks you as a glass business, and your Google Business Profile is set up. Rankings vary, but each page gives you another chance to show up.

What photos should I send?

Finished installs shot at an angle to cut reflections, with room lights on and the camera flash off. Close-ups of hardware, hinges and clean silicone lines help. Commercial jobs shot from the street look great. A few shots of broken glass are useful for the repairs page. I'll edit for glare where I can.

Can you make glass look good in photos?

I'll do my best with what you send, cropping and adjusting for glare and colour. The bigger win is shooting at an angle with some background visible through the glass, so people can see how clear it is. I'll send you a few quick tips before you start taking photos.

Should my site cover both homes and commercial work?

If you do both, yes. Homeowners want showers, splashbacks and pool fences. Builders and shop owners want shopfronts, partitions and reliable turnaround. I'll give each a clear section, so nobody has to sort through the wrong information to find what they need.

Can people send me measurements through the site?

Yes. The quote form can ask for the product, rough measurements, glass type and photos of the space. It won't replace a proper measure, but it lets you give a ballpark figure quickly and decide which jobs are worth the drive.
