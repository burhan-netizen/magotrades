Pricing questions

Straight answers.

How much does a website cost?

The build starts from $799 as a one-off payment. That covers up to 6 pages, every word written for you, Google Business Profile setup and a year of edits and support. Bigger builds are quoted before you commit.

Are there any monthly or yearly fees?

No monthly rent and no yearly fee from me. The only ongoing cost is your domain name, which you pay to your registrar directly, usually a small amount once a year. You own it, so it never depends on me.

What happens after the first year?

Your pick. Leave the site as it is and pay nothing. Send me changes at $25 an hour. Or update your photos, reviews and services yourself through the light CMS, free.

Why is the preview free?

Because you should see your website before you pay for it. I build a working preview from your Instagram or current site within 48 hours. If you do not love it, you walk away and owe nothing.

Who owns the website?

You do, outright, once it is paid for. Your domain, your code and your content. If you ever want to move it somewhere else, I hand everything over. No lock-in contract.

What could cost extra?

More than 6 pages, extra suburb or town pages, online booking, ongoing local SEO, the AI receptionist and social media. Everything is quoted in writing before you commit, so there are no surprises.
