Privacy policy

Short and plain. Last updated October 2026.

What I collect. Only what you send me: your name, email, phone number, business name, trade, country and message when you use the contact form, WhatsApp or email.

Why. To reply to you, prepare your free preview and, if you go ahead, build and support your website. I do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Where it goes. Messages arrive by email or WhatsApp. If an analytics tool is added to this site, it is used only to understand visits in aggregate.

How long. I keep enquiries for as long as needed to work with you, and you can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your rights. Ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you by emailing burhan@magolabs.in.
