Questions from the UK

Straight answers.

How much does a website for a tradesperson cost?

From £599, paid once. That covers six pages, custom design, all the writing, on-page SEO, Google Business Profile setup, a quote form, tap-to-call, your Instagram feed and a full year of edits. Extra pages, more locations or online booking are quoted before you commit. There's no monthly charge.

You're based in India. How does that work with a UK business?

Easily. We chat on WhatsApp, so you can send photos and voice notes from site. I'm a few hours ahead, which means quick replies in your working hours and plenty of work done before you've had your first brew. A fresh preview is often waiting for you in the morning.

Will the site use British spelling?

Always. Colour, organise, specialise, neighbour. I talk about quotes, postcodes and tradespeople, and use the phrases people type into Google, like "electrician near me" or "loft conversion Leeds". If anything reads oddly, tell me and I'll change it. It needs to sound like you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered to you and the code is yours. No contract tying you in, no monthly rent. If you ever want to move it elsewhere, you can. After year one, leave it as it is and pay nothing, or ask me for changes at £19 an hour.

How quickly can it go live?

Send me your Instagram or current site on WhatsApp and you'll have a free working preview within 48 hours, no strings. If you like it, we spend the next ten days or so on your photos, services and areas while I write the copy. Most sites go live on your own domain around day 14.
