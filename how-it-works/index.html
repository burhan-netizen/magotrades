How it works

Two weeks. Four steps. Zero homework.

You keep running jobs. I handle the rest. And you see your website working before you pay a cent.

Your time neededAbout 1 hour

In total, spread over two weeks. Mostly reading what I wrote and saying yes or no.

  1. Day 1

    Send me your Instagram

    Or your current website, or just a few photos of your jobs. Tell me your trade and where you work. Five minutes on WhatsApp is all I need to start.

  2. Within 48 hours

    See your site first

    I build a real, working preview of your website. Click through it, open it on your phone, show your partner, pull it apart. It is free and there are no strings. If it is not for you, you walk away.

  3. Days 3 to 13

    We make it yours

    Your photos, your services, your areas. I write every word, you tell me what sounds wrong. I set up your Google Business Profile, the local SEO basics and your contact forms.

  4. Day 14

    Live and ringing

    Launched on your own domain, tested on every phone, submitted to Google. Then you get a full year of edits and support, and every login is handed over to you.

Process questions

Straight answers.

What do you need from me to start?

Your Instagram or current website, or a handful of photos of your jobs. That is enough for me to build the free preview. Later I will ask a few quick questions about your services and areas over WhatsApp.

Do I have to write anything?

No. I write every word from a short chat with you. You read it, tell me what sounds wrong, and I fix it. Most owners spend less than an hour on their website in total.

What if I do not like the preview?

Then you walk away and owe nothing. The preview is free so you can judge the work before spending a cent.

How do payments work?

Nothing is paid for the preview. If you go ahead, the scope and price are agreed in writing first, and payment terms are set out before any work starts.

How long until it is live?

Usually about 14 days from go-ahead, as long as photos and feedback come back quickly. If you need it sooner, tell me and I will tell you honestly if it is possible.
