How much does a tradie website cost in New Zealand in 2026?

Most Kiwi tradies pay between NZ$1,000 and NZ$6,000 for a website built for them, with agency builds often higher. The range is wide because NZ pricing guides lump very different products together, from a budget package to a fully custom lead-generation site.

Going by NZ guides from 2025 and 2026, a starter small business site of 3 to 5 pages is around NZ$1,000 to NZ$3,000, and a standard 5 to 8 page site is around NZ$2,500 to NZ$6,000. Some agencies quote NZ$5,000 to NZ$15,000 for the same size of site, and custom lead-generating builds can reach NZ$9,000 to NZ$30,000.

At the cheap end, a few NZ providers sell fixed packages from around NZ$599, and Kiwi DIY builders offer pay-monthly plans from about NZ$15 a month. Nearly all NZ quotes are excluding GST, so add 15% if you are not GST registered.

What do the different options cost?

Here is a simple comparison in New Zealand dollars, before GST.

  • DIY builder (Wix, Squarespace, local builders like Dazzly or Rocketspark): roughly NZ$15 to NZ$40 a month, plus your own time.
  • Budget fixed package: around NZ$599 to NZ$1,200 for a simple site, usually with hosting charged monthly.
  • Freelancer or small studio: about NZ$1,000 to NZ$6,000 for a 3 to 8 page trade site.
  • Agency: about NZ$5,000 to NZ$15,000 or more for a small business site, and NZ$9,000 to NZ$30,000 for a custom lead-generation build.
  • Hosting and care plans: about NZ$25 to NZ$150 a month on top of most builds.
  • Mago Labs (my offer): NZ$1,449 one-off for up to 6 pages, no monthly fee.

Is a pay-monthly website worth it for a Kiwi tradie?

A pay-monthly website is worth it if the monthly fee is small and you are comfortable renting rather than owning. It gets poor value when the monthly fee is high and the site disappears the day you stop paying.

In New Zealand the pay-monthly market is a bit different from Australia. You will see DIY builders with low monthly fees (around NZ$15 a month for a basic plan), and agencies that bundle hosting, updates and support into care plans of roughly NZ$25 to NZ$150 a month on top of a build. Full done-for-you subscriptions with no upfront cost exist too, but compare them on a three-year total, not the monthly figure.

Ask whether there is a lock-in contract, and who owns the site and the domain if you leave. A good provider will answer both questions in writing without fuss.

What are the hidden and ongoing costs?

Plenty of NZ quotes look cheap until you add the yearly costs. These are the ones to plan for.

  • Domain name: a .co.nz or .nz usually costs about NZ$23 to NZ$50 a year, and renewal is often higher than the first-year price.
  • Hosting: about NZ$12 to NZ$60 a month for most small business sites.
  • Care plans: about NZ$40 to NZ$150 a month for updates, backups and support, mainly on WordPress.
  • GST: 15% on top of most quotes, plans and renewals if you are not GST registered.
  • Changes after launch: often charged hourly, so small edits like new photos or a new service can add up.
  • Platform fees: if you sell online through Shopify, its own monthly fees and app costs come on top.

What does a tradie website cost over three years?

Looking at three years gives a fair comparison between paying upfront and paying monthly. These are rough totals before GST.

  • DIY builder at NZ$15 a month: about NZ$540 over three years, plus your time.
  • Budget package at NZ$599 plus NZ$12.90 a month hosting: about NZ$1,064 over three years.
  • Studio build at NZ$3,000 plus a NZ$59 a month care plan: about NZ$5,124 over three years.
  • Agency build at NZ$6,000 plus a NZ$99 a month care plan: about NZ$9,564 over three years.
  • Mago Labs at NZ$1,449 plus a .co.nz domain: about NZ$1,520 to NZ$1,600 over three years, plus any changes you pay for after year one.

Do I need a website if I get work through Builderscrack or NoCowboys?

Most tradies still benefit from their own website, even if jobs come through Builderscrack, NoCowboys or word of mouth. Those platforms are useful, but your profile lives on their site, next to other tradies competing for the same job.

Your own website is where people land when they search your business name after a referral, and it can show up for local searches like "electrician Hamilton" or "roofer Christchurch". Link to your platform profiles and Google reviews from your site so people can see your track record in one place.

What should a tradie website include?

A tradie website needs to tell someone on their phone, quickly, what you do, where you work, and how to get a quote.

Include a page for each main service (a builder's decks and bathroom renovations are different searches), the towns and suburbs you cover, photos of your own jobs, a tap-to-call button and a short quote form. If you are a Licensed Building Practitioner, a registered electrician or a registered plumber, say so and give your licence or registration number so customers can check the public register.

Behind the scenes, ask for proper page titles, local business schema, a sitemap submitted to Google Search Console, and a Google Business Profile linked to the site. These cost little at build time and make a real difference to whether Google understands where you work.

Before you pay anyone

Questions to ask first.

  • Is the price plus GST or including GST?
  • Who owns the domain, and is it registered in my name?
  • Do I keep the website and content if I stop paying?
  • Is there a lock-in contract or a cancellation fee?
  • How many pages are included, and who writes the words?
  • What will hosting, care plans and changes cost after launch?
  • Will you set up my Google Business Profile and Search Console?
  • Can I see a working preview or a live tradie site you have built before paying in full?

Quick answers

Common questions.

How much should a tradie spend on a website in NZ?

For a solid 3 to 8 page site, NZ$1,000 to NZ$6,000 plus GST is a fair range in 2026. Agencies often quote NZ$5,000 to NZ$15,000 or more.

How much does a .co.nz domain cost?

Usually about NZ$23 to NZ$50 a year, with renewals often costing more than the first year. Register it in your own name.

Are NZ website prices quoted with GST?

Most are quoted excluding GST. Add 15% if you are not GST registered and cannot claim it back.

Is a DIY website good enough for a tradie?

It can be, and NZ builders start around NZ$15 a month. The real cost is the time it takes to write good service pages and set up Google properly.

Do I need a website if I use Builderscrack?

It helps. Your own site is yours, can show up in local Google searches, and gives customers a place to check you out after a referral or a platform lead.

Should I show my LBP or registration number on my website?

It is a good idea. Customers can check it on the public register, and it signals that you are properly licensed for the work.
