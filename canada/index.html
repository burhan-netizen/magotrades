If your website is slow, dated or missing, those calls go to whoever shows up first on their phone, and that window doesn't stay open for long.

Own it, do not rent it

HomeStars leads are rented. Your site is owned.

HomeStars, Jiffy and Yelp can send work your way, but you're renting those leads. The same homeowner is often talking to a few contractors at once, and the job leans toward whoever comes in cheapest. Stop paying and the leads stop, because the profile and the customer were never really yours. Your own website changes that. People find you on Google, look through your work and call you directly, with nobody else bidding on the same job. It keeps bringing in calls without a monthly invoice.

Questions from Canada

Straight answers.

How much does a contractor website cost in Canada?

From $1,149, paid once. That includes six pages, custom design, all the writing, on-page SEO, Google Business Profile setup, a quote form, tap-to-call, your Instagram feed and a full year of edits. Extra pages, more service areas or online booking are quoted up front. No monthly fees.

You're in India. How does that work for a Canadian contractor?

Better than you'd think. We work over WhatsApp, so you can send photos and voice notes straight from the job. I reply quickly during your business hours, and the time difference means a lot of the work happens overnight. Most mornings you'll wake up to progress, not a to-do list.

Will the wording fit a Canadian business?

Yes. I use the words your customers use: estimates, renos, provinces, licensed and insured, and the searches they type, like "deck builder near me" or "roofing contractor Calgary". If you prefer certain spellings or terms, tell me and I'll match them. It should sound like your business, start to finish.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the code is yours. No lock-in, no rent. If you want to move it later, you can take it with you. After the first year, leave it as it is and pay nothing, or bring me in for changes at $35 an hour.

How fast can my site go live?

Send your Instagram or current site on WhatsApp and you'll get a free working preview within 48 hours, no strings. If you like it, we spend the next ten days or so on your photos, services and areas while I write the copy. Most sites go live on your own domain around day 14.
