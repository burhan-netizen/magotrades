Questions from Australia

Straight answers.

How much does a tradie website cost?

From $1,149, paid once. That covers six pages, custom design, all the writing, Google setup, a quote form, tap-to-call, your Instagram feed and a full year of edits. If you need extra pages, more locations or online booking, I quote it before you commit. No monthly fee after that.

You're based in India. How does that work for an Aussie business?

Pretty smoothly. We talk on WhatsApp, so you can send photos and voice notes from the job site. I'm only a few hours behind, so replies come during your working day. A lot of the building happens while you're asleep, and a fresh preview is usually ready when you wake up.

Will it sound Australian or American?

Australian. I write colour, organise and licence, talk about suburbs, quotes and tradies, and use the phrases locals type into Google, like "concreter near me" or "emergency plumber Penrith". If anything reads off, tell me and I'll fix it. It's your business, so it should sound like you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the code is yours. There's no lock-in and no rent. If you want to move it somewhere else down the track, you can. After the first year you can leave it as is and pay nothing, or bring me in for changes.

How fast can it go live?

Send me your Instagram or current site on WhatsApp and you'll have a free working preview within 48 hours, no strings. If you like it, we spend the next ten days or so on your photos, services and suburbs while I write the copy. Most sites go live on your domain around day 14.
