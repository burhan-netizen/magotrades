LC Property Solutions: a website for property repairs in Wasatch Front, Utah

LC Property Solutions handle repairs and maintenance along Utah's Wasatch Front. Their customers usually have a list of small jobs they keep putting off, and the hard part is getting someone to come out for them.

Open the live siteSee all the work
Their headline

“That thing you keep meaning to get to? We got to it.”

A concept build: designed and written by Mago Labs for LC Property Solutions, using their real work and their own words.

What the site had to do

The site turns that list into a booking, and keeps the tone light while still feeling dependable.

The look: Forest green with a bright yellow accent, friendly and approachable.

It is built the same way as every Mago Labs site: hand-coded, fast on a phone, with tap-to-call on every page and the groundwork Google needs to find it.

Built in

Three things that make it work.

01

To-do list builder

Visitors tick off the jobs they need done, like a sticking door, a leaky tap or a fence panel, and send the whole list in one go. It turns several small jobs into one worthwhile visit.

02

Video before and afters

Short videos of real repairs show the difference the team makes, room by room.

03

Fun, and still serious

The writing has a sense of humour, but the details that matter, like insurance, pricing and how booking works, are clear and straightforward.
