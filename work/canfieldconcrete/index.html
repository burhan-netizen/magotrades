Concept website / Concrete / Utah

Canfield Construction: a website for concrete in Utah

Canfield Construction pour residential and commercial concrete in Utah. Concrete is a one-shot job, so clients want proof that the crew gets it right the first time.

Open the live siteSee all the work
Their headline

“Poured once. Poured right. Flat for decades.”

A concept build: designed and written by Mago Labs for Canfield Construction, using their real work and their own words.

What the site had to do

The site turns that idea of getting it right first time into something visitors can see and even play with, while giving them real tools to plan their own job.

The look: Dark charcoal and sandstone with a copper accent, calm and premium.

It is built the same way as every Mago Labs site: hand-coded, fast on a phone, with tap-to-call on every page and the groundwork Google needs to find it.

Built in

Three things that make it work.

01

Draw in wet concrete

A playful touch: visitors can drag a finger across a wet concrete surface and leave a mark, a small moment that people remember and share.

02

Slab calculator

Enter the slab size and thickness to estimate the concrete needed, which helps people arrive at a quote conversation already prepared.

03

Pour-day video

Real footage of a pour shows the crew, the equipment and the care taken, which builds trust faster than photos alone.
