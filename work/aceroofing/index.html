Ace Roofing Service: a website for roofing in Brisbane, QLD

Ace Roofing Service fix and replace roofs across Brisbane, where summer storms and hail send people searching for a roofer in a hurry. Most visitors arrive worried, often with water coming in.

Open the live siteSee all the work
Their headline

“Brisbane's roof worries, sorted. Rain, hail or shine.”

A concept build: designed and written by Mago Labs for Ace Roofing Service, using their real work and their own words.

What the site had to do

The site calms that worry first, helps people describe the problem in their own words, and makes the emergency number and the guarantee impossible to miss.

The look: Brick red with warm cream, friendly and local rather than corporate.

It is built the same way as every Mago Labs site: hand-coded, fast on a phone, with tap-to-call on every page and the groundwork Google needs to find it.

Built in

Three things that make it work.

01

"What's your roof doing?" checker

Visitors pick what they can see, like a leak, cracked tiles or rust, and get a plain explanation of what it probably means and what to do next. It turns panic into a clear next step.

02

10-year guarantee

The workmanship guarantee is stated clearly with what it covers, because a long guarantee is one of the strongest reasons to choose one roofer over another.

03

24/7 call-out

A tap-to-call button for emergencies stays in reach on every phone screen, so someone with a leak at night can reach a person in one tap.
