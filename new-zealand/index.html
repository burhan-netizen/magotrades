Questions from New Zealand

Straight answers.

How much does a tradie website cost in NZ?

From $1,449, paid once. That covers six pages, custom design, all the writing, on-page SEO, Google Business Profile setup, a quote form, tap-to-call, your Instagram feed and a full year of edits. Extra pages, more locations or online booking are quoted before you commit. No monthly fees.

You're based in India. Does that work for a Kiwi business?

It does. We talk on WhatsApp, so you can send photos and voice notes from the job. I reply quickly in your business hours, and because I'm behind you, a lot of the work happens while you're asleep. Most mornings there's something new to look at when you check your phone.

Will it sound like a New Zealand business?

Yes. I write colour, organise and licence, talk about tradies, quotes and suburbs, and use the searches locals type, like "builder near me" or "plumber Tauranga". If a word or phrase doesn't sit right with you, tell me and I'll change it. It should sound like you and your crew.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is in your name and the code is yours. No lock-in and no rent. If you want to move it somewhere else later, you can take it with you. After the first year, leave it and pay nothing, or get me in for changes at $45 an hour.

How quickly can it go live?

Send me your Instagram or current site on WhatsApp and you'll have a free working preview within 48 hours, no strings. If you're keen, we spend the next ten days or so on your photos, services and areas while I write the copy. Most sites go live on your own domain around day 14.
