Google Business Profile

Look sharp on the map.

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a customer sees, before your website and before your van. I set it up properly, or clean up the one you have, so it works as hard as you do.

WhatsApp meSee pricing
Pricing

Included free with every website build.

When someone searches "roofer near me", the map results usually show up first. Those little listings with photos, reviews and a call button are Google Business Profiles. Plenty of trades have one that was set up in five minutes years ago, with the wrong category, no services listed and three blurry photos. That costs calls you never know about.

I go through your profile line by line. The right primary and secondary categories, every service you offer, the areas you actually cover, real photos of your work and a link back to your website. Then I give you a simple way to ask happy customers for reviews. It comes free with every website build, because the two work best together.

What it does

What you get from google business profile.

Set up or cleaned up properly

Whether you are starting fresh or fixing an old listing, I check every field: business name, hours, phone number, description and verification. Small mistakes, like a mismatched phone number, can quietly hold your listing back in local results.

The right categories

Your primary category matters more than most people realise. "Roofing contractor" and "roofer" are not treated the same by Google. I pick the main one carefully and add secondary categories that match the work you actually want more of.

Services and service areas listed

Each service gets added with a short, clear description, and your service area is set to the towns and suburbs you really cover. Customers see straight away whether you do their job in their part of town.

Photos that show real work

Listings with real job photos get more attention than ones with a logo and nothing else. I help you pick your best before and after shots, finished jobs and your crew on site, and get them uploaded and organised.

Reviews and a link to your site

I set you up with a short review link you can text to customers after a job, plus a simple message to send with it. Your profile links to your website, so people can check your work before they call.

Who it is for

Honest fit.

This is for any trade business that gets work locally, especially if your listing is old, half finished or missing altogether. It suits owners who want it done right once and then just keep reviews coming in. It is not a review-buying service and it will not fake a shopfront address. If you work from home, I set it up the way Google allows.

Questions

Straight answers.

I work from home. Can I still have a profile?

Yes. Google lets service businesses hide their address and show a service area instead. Most trades work this way because they go to the customer. I set it up so your home address stays private and your listing shows the towns and suburbs you cover, which is exactly what customers need to see.

Can you get me more reviews?

I can make it much easier for you to ask. You get a short review link and a friendly message you can text to customers after a job. I never buy, write or fake reviews. Google removes those and can penalise your listing, and they are not worth the risk to your name.

My profile is already set up. Is it worth fixing?

Usually, yes. Most profiles I look at have something holding them back: the wrong main category, missing services, an outdated phone number or no recent photos. A clean-up often takes less time than a fresh setup, and you keep the reviews you have already earned.

Is it really free with a website build?

Yes. Your website and your Google profile work best as a pair, so I set up or clean up your profile as part of every build at no extra cost. If you only want the profile sorted without a new website, ask me and I will quote it before you commit.
