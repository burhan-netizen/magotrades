Website design for trades

A website that gets your phone ringing.

I build custom websites for trade businesses, coded by hand and written word for word. Six pages, live in 14 days, and you own every bit of it.

WhatsApp meSee pricing
Pricing

From the one-off build price, with no monthly rent.

From $799

Most trade websites fall into two camps. A cheap template that looks like every other builder in town, or an agency job that costs a fortune and still loads slowly on a phone. I do neither. I code your site from scratch, write every word, and set it up so the people searching for your work in your area can find you and call you in one tap.

You deal with me from the first WhatsApp message to the day it goes live. No account managers, no handoffs. Before Mago Labs I spent 8+ years in banking, fintech and insurance, so I look at a website the way you look at a quote: what does it cost, and what does it bring in? Pretty is nice. Booked jobs pay the bills.

What it does

What you get from website design for trades.

Built for the phone in a customer's hand

Most of your customers find you on a phone, often standing in a wet backyard or next to a leaking ceiling. The site loads fast, reads easily with one thumb, and puts your number where they can tap it straight away.

Hand-coded, no page builder

No WordPress plugins to update, no page builder slowing everything down, no theme someone else is also using. Clean code means faster loading, fewer things to break and a site that still works properly years from now.

Every word written for you

You send me your Instagram or current site and we have a short chat. I write the homepage, service pages and about page in plain language your customers understand, so you never stare at a blank page wondering what to say.

Set up for Google from day one

Page titles, descriptions, headings, schema markup and fast loading are all done properly before launch. Your Google Business Profile gets set up and linked too, so search engines know exactly what you do and where you work.

Turns visitors into calls and quotes

Tap-to-call buttons, a short quote form that lands in your inbox, and your best job photos up front. Every page has one clear next step, so a homeowner comparing three quotes picks up the phone to you.

Who it is for

Honest fit.

This is for trade owners who are good at the work and tired of losing jobs to competitors with better websites. It suits solo operators and small crews who want something done properly without managing it. It is not for anyone wanting a hundred-page site, an online shop, or the cheapest option going. If you just want a template, a DIY builder will do.

Questions

Straight answers.

How does the 14-day timeline work?

On day one you send me your Instagram or current site on WhatsApp. Within 48 hours you get a free working preview of your new website, no strings attached. From day three I gather your photos, services and areas, write every word and set up your Google profile. On day 14 it goes live on your own domain.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. You pay a one-off build fee and that is it. There is no monthly rent and no yearly fee. After the first year of edits and support, you can leave it as it is for nothing, pay a small hourly rate for changes, or update photos, reviews and services yourself through a simple editor.

What if I need more than six pages?

Plenty of businesses fit nicely into six pages: home, services, a few key service pages, about and contact. If you want extra service pages, separate pages for each town or suburb you cover, or online booking, I quote it up front so you know the full price before you commit.

Will I actually own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the code is yours. If you ever want to move to another developer, you can take everything with you. I would rather keep you because the site works than because you are locked in.
