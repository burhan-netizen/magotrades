Social media for trades

Your best work, posted while you keep working.

Customers check your Instagram or Facebook before they call. I turn your finished jobs into posts and reels so your pages look active, current and worth trusting.

WhatsApp meSee pricing
Pricing

Priced to the job, quoted before you commit.

You already have the content. Every finished driveway, new roof, fresh paint job or cleared block is proof you do good work. The problem is finding time to post it. So your last post was eight months ago, and a customer checking you out wonders if you are still trading. That doubt costs you calls.

Here is how it works. You send me your job photos and short videos straight from your phone, as rough as you like. I pick the best, write the captions and turn them into posts and reels that suit your trade. Your Instagram and Facebook stay active, your website feed updates with them, and people see recent work before they ever pick up the phone.

What it does

What you get from social media for trades.

Finished jobs turned into posts

Before and after shots, close-ups of the detail work, the crew on site. I sort through what you send, choose the photos that show your work best and turn them into clean posts with short captions that explain the job.

Short reels from phone footage

A ten-second clip of a concrete pour or a slow pan across a new deck makes a strong reel. I cut your rough phone footage into short videos with captions, ready for Instagram and Facebook, without you learning any editing apps.

Captions that sound like you

No hashtag soup or cheesy sales lines. Captions are short and plain: what the job was, where it was, what was tricky about it. They read like a tradesperson proud of the work, because that is what they are.

Keeps your pages active

A steady rhythm of posts shows customers you are busy, current and still trading. When someone finds you on Google and taps through to your Instagram, they see recent jobs, which gives them confidence to call.

Ties into your website

Your Instagram feed can show on your website, so every new post refreshes your site too. Customers see fresh work in both places, and you only had to send a few photos from the job.

Who it is for

Honest fit.

This suits trades doing visual work like concreting, landscaping, roofing, tiling or building, where a good photo sells the job. It works best if you can snap a few photos and clips each week. It is not paid advertising, follower buying or a viral content promise. If you never take photos on site, there is not much for me to work with.

What is included

  • Posts made from your job photos
  • Short reels cut from phone footage
  • Captions written in your voice
  • A regular posting rhythm agreed upfront
  • Instagram and Facebook covered
  • Simple photo tips for future jobs
  • Instagram feed shown on your website
  • A monthly check on what worked

Questions

Straight answers.

What do I need to send you?

Photos and short videos from your phone, straight after a job or at the end of the week. Before and after shots are gold. Rough is fine, I sort out the cropping and order. A quick voice note about the job helps me write a caption that gets the details right.

Will this get me more followers?

It might, but followers are not really the goal. The aim is that when a customer finds you and checks your pages, they see recent, real work and trust you enough to call. A busy, current page does that far better than a big follower count with nothing new on it.

Do you run paid ads too?

This service covers your regular posts and reels, not paid advertising. If you want to try ads later, we can talk about whether they make sense for your trade and area. For many local trades, a steady flow of real job posts plus a good website and Google profile does plenty.

How often will you post?

We agree a rhythm upfront based on how much work you send and what you want to spend. Consistency matters more than volume. A few good posts a week from real jobs beats daily filler. I will quote the job before you commit, so there are no surprises.
